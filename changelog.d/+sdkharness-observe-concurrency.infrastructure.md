The repository-owned sdkharness conformance checks `large.concurrent_parts` and `large.parallel_download` now observe real concurrency (overlapping `b2_upload_part` requests and overlapping download streams including ranged GETs) through a loopback observing proxy instead of only comparing sizes and bytes, and `large.concurrent_parts` also downloads and verifies the uploaded bytes. The resilience executable now follows `LATEST_STABLE_VERSION`, accepts `contentSha1: none` only for large files, names its scenario in the result line when the configuration is refused, and retries only its own authorize setup step. All executables scrub proxy variables from the CLI environment, and unit tests cover the loopback and fixed-credential guards. This also records that the repository-owned conformance and resilience checks moved into `.sdkharness/` by #1171 (bucket), #1172 (encryption), #1173 (file lock), #1174 (client and key), #1175 (large-transfer and URL), #1176 and #1177 (upload resilience) were merged without individual changelog fragments.
