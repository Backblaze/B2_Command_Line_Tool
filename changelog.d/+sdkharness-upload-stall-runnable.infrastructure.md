The sdkharness `upload.stall` resilience check (`.sdkharness/tests/resilience-upload.py`) now runs instead of reporting `SKIP no-client-option`: it sets `CI` and `GITHUB_REPOSITORY` in the CLI's environment for that scenario only, which activates the short request timeouts the CLI already applies in its own CI (`b2/_internal/_cli/b2api.py`), so a 60 s stalled upload is abandoned and retried on a new upload URL.
